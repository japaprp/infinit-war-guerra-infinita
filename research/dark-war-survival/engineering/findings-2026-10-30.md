**Scope:** read-only inventory of installed Android client assets and static client metadata.  
**Confidence:** client-side artifacts only; no backend implementation or live server behavior is established.

## 2. Gameplay system inventory

- Client references cover hero quality/rank, new-hero definitions, level-up, preview skills, enhancement, exclusive equipment, hero skills/effects.
- Lua modules cover hero data, equipment, intensification, level-up templates, weapons, recruitment and detail UI.

### Equipment and vehicles
- References include hero equipment, exclusive equipment, vehicle equipment/sets, equipment attributes/materials/promotion/upgrade.
- Separate families exist; do not assume shared progression curves or identical combat applicability.
- Exact slots, rarity rules, costs and bonuses: NOT VERIFIED.

### Skills, buffs, formations and battles

### Competitive modes
- Client references include Arena, Arena 3v3, Champion Battle, World Cup schedule/betting, Strongest Kingdom phases, and Wasteland King ranking/level.
- Live availability, eligibility, scoring, brackets, entry limits, rewards and reset rules: NOT VERIFIED.

### World map, alliance and seasons
- References include world scenes/cities, city colors, fortresses, alliance towers, monster refresh, quests, restrictions, troop movement, rallies, cross-server movement, season configuration/rewards/influence and seasonal map systems.
- Tile ownership, movement speed, terrain modifiers, protection, season reset and inter-server transfer rules: NOT VERIFIED.

### Base, buildings, research and resources

### PvE and monsters
- Multiple campaign, PvE, trial, nightmare, zombie, monster and event configuration families exist.
- Do not assume a single difficulty curve or shared hero/troop rules.

## 4. On-demand asset evidence
The separate download index references a ben.prefab bundle not found in the device AssetBundles cache snapshot; the corresponding small bundle was available in the installed package. This supports a separate indexed/download asset path for at least one item. It does not prove every hero or asset is dynamically downloaded.

## 5. Season and map evidence
- World-terrain folders were identified for S0, S1, S4, S5, S6 and S7 in the selected gameres subtree.
- Selected map/season extraction includes 42 map-terrain bundles, 8 S5 scene bundles, 3 S7 scene bundles and one small on-demand hero prefab.
- Client catalog references seasonal UI families S4–S7 and battle-pass art S0–S7.
- No S2/S3 folder in the inspected subtree is not proof those seasons never existed.
- S7 client references do not prove exact S7 rules or live availability on a particular zone.

## 6. Explicit non-findings
This work does not establish:
1. Server language, framework, database schema or live endpoints.
2. Server-side authoritative combat, matchmaking, economy, RNG or anti-cheat logic.
3. Exact formulas for hero progression, equipment, buildings, research, troops, seasonal scoring or combat.
4. Live feature availability for a specific account, alliance, zone or season.
5. Whether client references are active, dormant, legacy or feature-flagged.

No undocumented/private endpoint probing, traffic interception, account/session inspection, or game modification is part of this work.
