## 6. Version and source caveats
- CheckVersion.txt was observed as 1.250.669;1629; local index header was #30491.250.670.
- Exact build alignment is NOT VERIFIED.
- The installed package inventory was taken from a split APK. The full ~1.9 GB APK was not copied into the analysis folder; only its ZIP directory was listed and selected bundles were streamed read-only.
- Absence from the phone cache alone is not proof that an asset is unavailable in the installed package or cannot be obtained through normal game operation.
